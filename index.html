About me

More than a résumé

An electrical engineer who started out building robots and IoT products, and now leads enterprise integration programmes for global clients.

I'm a PMP and CSM certified delivery leader with more than 10 years of experience managing technology projects and programmes. At NTT DATA, I direct a USD 20M portfolio of 20+ MuleSoft, Salesforce and ERP integration projects for clients in the US and UK. The portfolio spans 7 industries, including banking, e-commerce, construction, telecommunications and automotive services.

My career began at Altsense Technology Solutions, where I managed embedded systems and robotics projects. That work ranged from building management systems to a food-serving robot. Getting hardware, firmware and software teams to deliver together taught me the fundamentals: clear scope, honest status reporting and steady client communication.

I enjoy recovering accounts that are in trouble. My flagship global e-commerce account went from minus 30% to a sustained plus 70% margin, one of the strongest recoveries in our Business Unit. I've also brought escalated accounts back to healthy, high-satisfaction status within two months.

Lately I've been focused on AI in delivery. I wrote "AI-Adopted Agile Project Management: The Time Saver", which won NTT DATA's Best Technical Paper award, and I help delivery teams adopt Generative AI tools. I'm open to relocating to the UAE for my next role.
